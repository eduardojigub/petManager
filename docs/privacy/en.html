Pet's Life

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Português

This policy describes how the Pet's Life app handles information. The person responsible for the app is Eduardo Sousa. For questions, corrections, or deletion, email eduardobrunoit@gmail.com.

The app is on the Apple App Store with id 6737129802 and on Android with package name com.petmanager.cat.

1. What this policy covers

Pet's Life lets you keep information about your pets: profile, health records, schedule, and expenses, including photos you choose to upload. There is no separate account website. What the app does not do is in What we do not collect.

2. Data the app collects and stores

An account is required. Without signing in, the pet area does not open. Sign-up is email and password, through Google Firebase Authentication.

Account

  • Email address.
  • Password. It is sent to Firebase Authentication to create the account, sign in, change the password, confirm deletion, and, if you ask, reset the password. The app does not store the password in the pet database or keep it on the device for later reading.
  • Display name, only if you save a name in Settings → Account → edit profile. If you do not save a name, the app shows the part of the email before the @ on screen without storing that text as a name.
  • Profile photo, only if you pick an image from the gallery. The file goes to Firebase Storage and the image address is stored on the authentication profile.
  • Account identifier generated by Firebase Authentication (the uid).

If you ask to reset your password, Firebase sends a reset email to the address you entered. The app does not send a newsletter. Email schedule reminders are not used: that field is always saved as off.

Pet profile

When you save a pet, the app stores this in Cloud Firestore, tied to the account uid:

  • Name, breed, birthday, age calculated from that date, and weight. These are required to save the pet.
  • The pet's sex (male or female), color, and microchip number, if you fill them in. They are optional. The sex is the pet's, not yours.
  • Pet photo, if you pick an image from the gallery. The file goes to Firebase Storage, in a folder for your account, and the profile stores the image address.

Pet health records

These are notes you write about the pet. The app does not read Apple Health, HealthKit, or exercise data.

  • Type: vaccine, vet appointment, medication, grooming, or other.
  • Description, date, and notes.
  • Photo, if you pick an image from the gallery.
  • A due date, if you enter one, and the on-device reminder choice (on or off, and how many days before).
  • Depending on the type, if you fill them in: vet name, clinic name, weight at the visit, vaccine batch, dosage, frequency, and grooming services.
  • The local reminder identifier, when a reminder is scheduled on this device.

Schedule

  • Type, description, date, and time.
  • Whether you asked for a local reminder, and how many minutes before.
  • That reminder's identifier on the device, when one exists.

Expenses

  • Title, amount, type (food, medical, grooming, toys, or other), and date.
  • Whether the expense repeats, and the frequency: weekly, monthly, or yearly.

The amount is the number you type for your own records. The app does not take a card, process a payment, or sell an in-app purchase.

Technical account record

The app may store a technical document with the uid, only to mark that older data for the account has been tied to that owner. It is not a profile and it is not included in the export PDF.

What stays on the device only

  • Which pet is selected.
  • Whether local reminders are on.
  • The app language (Portuguese or English). This item stays on the device after account deletion.
  • A local mark that ownership of the data was already checked on this install.
  • The reminders themselves. They are local notifications. The text can include the type, the description, and the pet's name. The app does not request a remote push token and does not send these reminders to a push server.

3. What we do not collect

As the app works today, we do not collect:

  • Location.
  • Contacts, the device calendar, or SMS.
  • Audio or video. The image picker accepts gallery images only.
  • An advertising identifier, or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Browsing or search history.
  • Your health or fitness data.
  • Payment data.
  • Crash reports, analytics, or ads. The app does not include an analytics SDK, a crash-reporting SDK, an ads SDK, or an advertising-tracking SDK. This app's Firebase configuration has analytics and ads turned off. In the iOS project, the Expo update check is turned off.

The iOS project declares camera and microphone permission text, and the Android configuration declares the audio-recording permission. The app code does not open the camera, does not record audio, and does not request those permissions at runtime. They are not used to collect data.

4. Why we use the data

  • To create the account, sign in, change the password, and confirm it is you when you delete the account.
  • To store and show the pets, health records, schedule, expenses, and photos you saved.
  • To schedule, on the device, the reminders you turned on.
  • To build, on the device, a PDF summary of your data when you ask to export it.
  • To delete the account and the data tied to it when you ask.

We do not use this data for advertising, ad profiling, or sale to third parties. The app has no ad personalization and no audience measurement.

5. Where the data lives

  • Account (email, password credential, display name, profile photo, and uid): Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Profiles, health records, schedule, expenses, and the technical record: Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Photos uploaded by this version of the app: Google Cloud Storage for Firebase, in a folder for your account.
  • Preferences and reminders: on the device.

We do not run a separate server besides Firebase. What the app sends to Firebase goes over HTTPS. The app has no automatic deadline that deletes data. It stays while the account exists and you have not deleted the item, except for the deletion described below.

6. Third parties

The service that receives the account, the records, and the photos is Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage), to host the app. Google processes that data to provide that service.

The app's other libraries (navigation, fonts bundled in the app, icons, and on-screen charts) do not receive the account or those records.

When you export the PDF or share the store link, the device opens the system share sheet. The destination is your choice. Pet's Life does not receive the list of destinations.

7. Permissions the app requests

  • Photos. Requested when you pick a gallery image for your profile, a pet, or a health record.
  • Notifications. Requested when the app opens, and again if you turn reminders on in Settings. They are for local reminders.

8. Export and access

In Settings, Export Data builds a PDF on the device and opens the share sheet. For each pet on the account, the PDF includes name, breed, age, and weight; health records (date, type, description, and notes); schedule (date, time, type, and description); and expenses (date, type, title, and amount). It does not include the account email or the photo files. The app does not send that PDF to the developer.

You can also see and edit the data in the app. For a copy or a correction you cannot make yourself, email eduardobrunoit@gmail.com.

9. Deletion

You can delete a pet, a health record, a schedule entry, or an expense on the matching screens. That removes that item in Firestore. Deleting a health record or a schedule entry also cancels that item's local reminder. Deleting a pet removes the pet profile. That action alone does not delete health records, the schedule, expenses, or the photo file.

To delete the account and the data tied to it:

  1. Open Settings (in Portuguese, Ajustes).
  2. Tap Account (in Portuguese, Conta).
  3. Tap Delete my account (in Portuguese, Excluir minha conta).
  4. Enter your password and confirm.

Deletion confirms the password, deletes the account documents the app can find (profiles, health, schedule, expenses, and the technical record), deletes Storage photos still linked to those documents or to the profile photo, cancels local reminders, clears the account's local preferences, and then deletes the login. The language chosen on the device stays.

A photo whose record was already deleted may not be found by that process, because deletion removes files that are still linked to the account's data. If deletion cannot finish, the account stays active so you can try again.

If you cannot do it in the app, email eduardobrunoit@gmail.com from the account email and ask for deletion. We use that contact to find the account and complete the request.

10. Children

Pet's Life is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe a child created an account, email eduardobrunoit@gmail.com and ask for deletion.

11. Security

The account uses email and password. Data sent to Firebase goes over HTTPS. The app ties records to the account uid and queries that account's records. No storage method removes every risk.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, the effective date at the top of this page changes, and the new version stays at this same address. If a change matters for data already collected, the change will also be noted on this page. Use of the app after the new version is published applies to the version then published.
